## The Story the Preview Sells: A Perfect Bride and a Suspicious Brother-in-Law

The official synopsis is unusually efficient. A bride who seems flawless arrives in a chaotic Meerut household and turns it into something close to paradise. Her brother-in-law, instead of relaxing, grows suspicious — and his suspicion hardens into a genuinely strange question: is the ideal daughter-in-law actually an evil force from another world?

That single sentence explains why the film's genre tags look like a committee wrote them. Nayi Naveli is filed under comedy, drama, family, fantasy, horror, and mystery — six categories that do not normally share a shelf.

The Meerut setting matters more than it might seem. Hindi cinema has spent years setting family comedies in Delhi, Punjab, and small-town Uttar Pradesh, and Meerut gives the film a specific texture — a tier-two city where a joint family, a new bride, and a supernatural problem can plausibly collide.
