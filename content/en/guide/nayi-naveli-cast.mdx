## How to Verify Nayi Naveli Cast Details Yourself

Credit pages change constantly between a film's announcement and its release, and secondary sites copy each other's errors. A few habits will keep you accurate.

- **Start with the primary credits page.** The [IMDb full cast and crew listing for Nayi Naveli](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt40855841/fullcredits/) is the most complete single source currently available, and it's updated as studio materials arrive.
- **Check transliterations.** Search both "Pratieks" and "Prateeks," and both "Pachori" and "Pachauri." Indian names are frequently romanized two ways across distributors.
- **Separate cast from crew.** Fan write-ups often blend the two, then attribute a producer's credit to an actor. Use the department headings as your filter.
- **Treat character names as provisional.** A role tag like "Office Colleague" is a placeholder in the credits, not necessarily the name used on screen.
- **Cross-check against trailers and posters.** Billing order on a poster tells you who leads the film; credits order in a database doesn't always match.
- **Date your notes.** A credit list you saved in one month may be outdated the next.
