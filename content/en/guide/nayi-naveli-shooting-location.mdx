### Reading the Clues in the Trailer

Trailers are the cheapest research tool available. Watch the full three-minute trailer and the shorter teaser, and pay attention to:

- **End cards** — production service companies and studio logos sometimes appear in the final frames.
- **Street signage** — Hindi versus English shop boards hint at a real UP location versus a dressed set.
- **Light quality** — hard, dusty daylight usually means genuine exterior shooting; soft, even light suggests a controlled floor.
- **Background crowds** — real bazaars are hard to fake and expensive to recreate.

None of these are proof. They are filters that help you discard bad claims fast.
