## What the Ending Is Really About

Strip away the horror and fantasy dressing and *Nayi Naveli* is a movie about how uncomfortable people get when something actually goes right.

- **Gratitude vs. suspicion.** The family gets what it wanted and immediately assumes there's a price.
- **The outsider who fixes things.** Every family has someone who arrives and reorganizes everything — and every family resents them a little.
- **The cost of being perfect.** The bride is punished for competence. That's a theme that resonates well beyond Meerut.
- **Who gets to define "evil."** The film's smartest move is letting the accusation come from a single suspicious relative rather than an objective narrator.

That's why the **Nayi Naveli ending explained** discussion keeps going. The movie isn't really hiding a twist — it's handing you a mirror and asking which side of it you're on.
