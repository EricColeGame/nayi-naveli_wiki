## What Is Nayi Naveli?

*Nayi Naveli* (also listed as *Nayyi Navelli*) is a Hindi-language comedy-drama with fantasy, horror, and mystery elements, directed by Balaji Mohan. Mohan co-wrote the screenplay with Divy Nidhi Sharma. The premise is a neat genre flip: a seemingly perfect bride arrives in a chaotic Meerut household and turns it into a domestic paradise, until her brother-in-law starts to suspect that the ideal daughter-in-law may be something far less human.

That "too good to be true" hook is the engine of the whole film. It borrows the warm, noisy family-comedy texture that Hindi audiences know well, then slowly tilts it toward supernatural horror.
