A few habits will keep you accurate:

- **Wait for corroboration.** One outlet reporting a number is a claim; three converging on a range is data.
- **Check the label.** "Estimated" and "confirmed" are different words for a reason.
- **Watch the trend, not the headline.** A soft opening with rising day-two numbers tells a better story than a single Friday figure.
- **Distinguish India net from worldwide gross.** They are not comparable, and mixing them produces nonsense comparisons.
