### Why the $674 Figure Needs Context

IMDb's box office panel pulls from distributor disclosures, and those disclosures are inconsistent for smaller Indian titles. Many films simply never report a final India nett figure, and international collections are often logged late or not at all.

There is also a currency issue. Indian trade reporting runs on rupee nett and gross figures, while IMDb's panel is denominated in US dollars. A single dollar figure with no rupee equivalent and no day-wise breakdown is a data point, not a box office story.

The practical takeaway: any viral "day-wise collection table" you see circulating on social media should be treated as unverified unless it cites a named trade outlet or an official distributor statement.

## Why the Nayi Naveli Box Office Collection Stayed Low

| Marketing challenge | Why it hurts collections |
|---|---|
| Horror-comedy framing | Splits the audience; horror fans want scares, family viewers want warmth |
| "Family" tag alongside "evil force" | Confuses the core promise in trailers |
| Small-town household setting | Travels well on streaming, less reliably in multiplexes |
| Star-led but not star-driven | Yami Gautam draws credibility, not opening-weekend crowds |

Audiences buy a clear promise. When a trailer has to communicate five genres in three minutes, the result is usually curiosity rather than commitment.

### Release and Distribution Strategy

If a film arrives on a major streaming platform shortly after a limited cinema window, theatrical collections are structurally capped. That is a distribution decision, not an audience verdict.

### Word of Mouth Never Compounded

The **Nayi Naveli box office collection** had no second-weekend catalyst. No viral moment, no standout song, no critic champion. In a market where a film's fate is decided between Friday and Monday, silence is fatal.
