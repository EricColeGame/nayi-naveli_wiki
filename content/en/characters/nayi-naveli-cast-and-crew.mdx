## How to Verify Cast and Crew Details Yourself

Public credit lists change constantly between a film's announcement and its release, so it's worth knowing where to check. Start with [IMDb's full cast and crew listing for Nayi Naveli](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt40855841/fullcredits/), which is the most complete single-page record available and is updated as credits are verified.

A few habits that save time:

1. **Check the "as" notes.** When a performer is credited as a variant spelling of their name — as Prateek Pachori is here — searching only the alternate spelling can make a credit look missing.
2. **Separate cast from crew visually.** Credits pages often list character tags in a separate column that gets flattened when copied elsewhere, which is how role mix-ups spread.
3. **Cross-check the production company.** A single executive credit naming a studio confirms which banner is actually behind the film.
4. **Ignore placeholder entries.** Early listings sometimes carry bracketed roles or asterisks instead of real character names; those are unfinished data, not reveals.
5. **Wait for the release window.** Final on-screen credits are the only authoritative source for who played whom.
